## 3. Multi-condition findings (condensed)

### 3.1 Segment angles (JTBD)
- **Pay:** Mid-size EPR manufacturers  
- **Move tonnes:** Soft-KYC shops + recycler-backed hubs  
- **Density wedge:** RWA / SMB — not mass Tier-2 consumers  
- **Never first:** Large OEMs, SPCB ARR, PG students as CAC engine  

### 3.2 Scenario angles (EPR × liquidity × density × consumer UPI)
- **Scale path:** High/Med EPR + shop liquidity YES + city density YES  
- **Fail path:** PRD defaults (no liquidity, no density, points-only)  
- **Control surfaces you own:** B (liquidity) and C (density). A (EPR) is weather. D (UPI) amplifies, doesn’t fund.

### 3.3 Competitive angles
- Don’t fight kabadi/Cashify on cash phones  
- Don’t fight Recykal on credit brokerage  
- Don’t fight Karo/Attero on mega-OEM programmes  
- **Open cell:** mid-size “show me the chain” evidence SaaS + city formal corridors  

### 3.4 Unit economics angles (illustrative)
- Points-only pickup: cheap P&L, dead volume  
- Shop: unshared freight + monthly settle → negative vs street  
- Hub: full truck works; half truck / thin tonnage dies  
- Mid-size SaaS: ~3–4× LTV:CAC **if** certs exist  

### 3.5 Regulatory angles
- Platform attestation ≠ CPCB EPR certificate  
- Partner PRO; never shadow PRO  
- Skip government dashboard for v1; public verify-by-number only  
- Theatre (fake PDF mill) is the company-kill scenario
