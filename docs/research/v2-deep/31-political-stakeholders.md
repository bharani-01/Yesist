**Who can kill it outright:** CM office (by not funding), UDHD/IMC (by running a parallel channel and denying ground access), and Environment dept (by not issuing the GO/direction). **Who can wound it publicly:** opposition + NGOs + media via the Pithampur or displacement frame, and scrap traders via boycott. **Most credible champions:** IMC Commissioner (if EcoSure earns Swachh Survekshan evidence), authorised recyclers (feedstock), MPPCB (CPCB reporting).

## 3. Findings

### F1. The PRD's sponsor model points at the weakest political actor
The PRD names "SPCB with the state IT department" as sponsor (00-overview §1, SP-01). In MP the Board is chaired ex officio by the Principal Secretary, Environment (P9), so the real decision-maker is the Environment department secretariat, not the Board. More importantly, the citizen-facing e-waste stream in Indore is owned politically by IMC and UDHD: the CM flagged off **IMC** e-waste vehicles with the UDHD minister and the mayor beside him (P8). A programme sponsored only by SPCB will look like a Bhopal regulator entering a ULB's flagship space. Without UDHD/IMC it cannot get vehicles, RRR centres, RWA access or ward data (R12 F2), and UDHD can quietly starve it.

### F2. The roadmap collides with the 2027 IMC election and the 2028 Assembly election
- If sponsorship is signed in Q4 2026, the roadmap (pilot 12 weeks, Phase 0 6–8 weeks, Phase 1 12–16 weeks; 13-roadmap) puts the **citizen go-live of Phase 1 around mid-2027**. That is the IMC election window: urban body elections are due in 2027 (P2, P3; the 2022 council took oath in Aug 2022, so the term runs to about Aug 2027). The exact poll month is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Under Model Code of Conduct norms, fresh sanctions, new beneficiary releases and scheme announcements stop, and completed schemes may only be commissioned by civil authority without fanfare (P14). A **new UPI citizen incentive** launched during the ULB code would be exactly the kind of beneficiary-oriented release the code restricts. Whether the MP State Election Commission applies identical rules is **UNVERIFIED**, but the risk is material.
- The mayoral seat goes to a reserved category by lottery (P2), so the incumbent mayor's political horizon is short and the next mayor, council and possibly commissioner will re-evaluate inherited projects in H2 2027.
- The Assembly election is expected in **Nov 2028** (P1), with the code typically from about Oct 2028. Phase 3 ("second corridor") falls in 2028. A second-corridor announcement in the pre-poll quarter is either impossible (code) or looks electoral.
- **Implication:** the pilot and Phase 0 should run before the ULB code, and the public citizen launch should either precede it or wait until the new council is seated. Swachhata Hi Seva (17 Sept–2 Oct 2027) is the natural public launch moment: it is after the new council, it is when the CM launched e-waste in 2025, and it fits a Phase 1 go-live.

### F3. Indore's political capital is damaged and its bureaucracy is churning
- The Bhagirathpura contaminated-water deaths (24 of 36 examined deaths linked to sewage-contaminated water by the judicial commission, P6) led to the commissioner's removal after about 4 months, suspensions, and opposition demands for the UDHD minister's resignation and an FIR against the mayor. The report remains sealed and the case is live in the High Court (P6).
- IMC has had three commissioners in roughly five months (Sept 2025–Jan 2026, P5). The mayor's fifth-year focus is roads and water, not e-waste (P4).
- **Implications:** (a) IMC leadership is defensive and will avoid any new "toxic" headline; (b) sponsorship tied to one officer will not survive a transfer, so it must be institutional (GO, MoU, named post, not named person); (c) there is also an opportunity: an honest, verifiable e-waste custody record is a "rebuild trust" story for a city whose Swachh brand took a hit (P7).

### F4. Pithampur is a live political fuse, not just a reputational note
The Carbide ash (~850–900 t) is still the subject of see-sawing High Court orders and organised local resistance (Pithampur Bachao Samiti; Bhopal gas activists), after mass protests and stone-pelting in Jan 2025 (P10, P11). A 2026 Sector 3 waste-unit fire added to it (R12 F1). Any EcoSure hub, storage godown, trip routing, or even the corridor name "Indore + Pithampur" gives the opposition and NGOs a ready frame: "more hazardous waste to Pithampur". Research 12 already recommends no Pithampur hub; the political evidence strengthens this to a **hard rule plus a communication line**, not a preference.

### F5. Scrap traders will read a custody trail as a tax trail
In March 2026 the DGGI probed a reported ₹100-crore fake-billing racket in Indore's iron and scrap trade, with office-bearers of the Indore Loha Vyapari Association reportedly "on radar" and Pithampur scrap traders raided (P12). Industry has asked for mandatory e-invoicing in scrap (P12). A government platform that records weights, parties and payments will be assumed to feed GST and police. Traders are organised and close to the ruling party (P12, **UNVERIFIED** in detail), so a quiet boycott plus lobbying is a realistic kill path. The PRD's data rules (09-government G9 "lawful data requests") do not say what is **not** shared, and research 14 R5 only covers worker data, not trader data.

### F6. Producers and recyclers are not a united "industry lobby"
Major producers are litigating the ₹22/kg EPR floor price in the Delhi HC, and the court stayed CPCB's price self-declaration in Dec 2025 (P13). That makes the PRD's "producer-funded take-back pool" (00-overview §1, SP-04) politically fragile: producers will not volunteer new costs while arguing costs are already too high. Recyclers gain from the floor price and want feedstock, so they are the natural champions. But Newslaundry's reporting on below-floor certificate trading (P13) means some recyclers will resist EcoSure's public verification and weight caps. The PRD should expect champion recyclers and resistant ones, and choose the corridor recycler accordingly.

### F7. The PRD has no governance, communication or political-risk layer
00-overview §10 lists operational risks only; "Programme abandoned mid-pilot" is mitigated only by stage gates. There is no steering committee, no named escalation path above the SPCB, no communication plan, no election-calendar constraint, no MCC-compliant branding rule, and no crisis playbook. Kill criteria (SP-09) are operational only. For a state programme in a city heading into municipal polls, this is the biggest structural gap.

### F8. The honesty principles are a political asset if used deliberately
"Formal network only" labels, public attestation verification and "custody attestation, not EPR certificate" naming (00-overview §5) are exactly what protect EcoSure from the "fake numbers" and "fake certificate" media frames (P13) and from Swachh Survekshan scepticism (P7). The PRD treats them as product rules; they should also be the core of the communication strategy.

## 4. Communication and sponsorship strategy (recommended)

**Sponsorship structure**
1. Joint GO from Environment department and UDHD naming EcoSure a state programme, with MPPCB as regulatory owner (recyclers, producers, flags) and IMC as operational co-sponsor (citizen collection, RRR centres, vehicles). MPSEDC named as IT agency.
2. Steering committee chaired by the ACS/PS Environment, with PS UDHD (or Commissioner Urban Administration), IMC Commissioner, MPPCB Member Secretary, MPSEDC MD, one recycler, one trader-association and one waste-picker-organisation representative. Meets monthly and signs every pilot gate. Membership is by post, so transfers do not break it.
3. Budget: platform and float from the Environment department scheme head; ULB operations from SBM-U 2.0 state share via UDHD (**UNVERIFIED** eligibility); producer pool optional, not a launch dependency, until the Delhi HC case settles.
4. Sanction timing: GO, budget sanction and operator contract signed **before** any ULB election code, so the programme is an "ongoing scheme" (P14).

**Election-aware timeline**
| Window | Activity | Political framing |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| Oct–Dec 2026 | GO, MoU with IMC, recycler checks, operator contract | Administrative, no launch event |
| Dec 2026–Mar 2027 | Manual pilot in IMC wards only | Civil-authority "trial", IMC Commissioner as spokesperson |
| Mar–Jun 2027 | Phase 0 build; no new citizen incentive announcements | Quiet |
| ULB code window (2027, month **UNVERIFIED**) | Continue ongoing collection only; no new incentives, no ribbon-cutting, no politician imagery in app | Code-compliant |
| After new council (Q3 2027) | Phase 1 public launch at Swachhata Hi Seva (17 Sep–2 Oct 2027), CM + new mayor | "Indore's e-waste now has a verified trail" |
| Q1–Q2 2028 | Phase 2 producers and SPCB views; second-corridor decision (Bhopal via Swachh City Partnership, R12) | State scale-up story |
| Jul–Dec 2028 | No new corridor launch during Assembly code (from ~Oct 2028); run steady state | Continuity, no announcements |

**Messages by audience**
- Citizens (Hindi first): "Your old phone, safely handed over, with a receipt you can check." Avoid "hazardous" and "toxic" in consumer copy; lead with data safety and UPI.
- Kabadis and traders: "Faster payment, same buyers, your data is not shared with tax or police without a court or statutory order." Onboard through associations and collectives, not individual sign-up drives.
- Pithampur: "No e-waste is stored or processed in Pithampur under this pilot. Material leaves Indore for authorised recyclers." Show it on the public verification page.
- IMC/UDHD: "EcoSure gives IMC ward-level, verifiable e-waste evidence for Swachh Survekshan and SWM Rules 2026 reporting." Credit goes to IMC.
- MPPCB/Environment: "The quarterly CPCB action-plan report, pre-filled."
- Media: publish monthly formal-network figures with the honesty label; never publish estimates of informal volume; give first access to the public verification page.

**Crisis playbook triggers**: a fire or accident at any EcoSure hub or recycler; a fraudulent attestation found; a displacement complaint going public; a Pithampur rumour. Each needs a named spokesperson (IMC Commissioner for city, MPPCB Member Secretary for regulatory), a 24-hour holding statement, and the ability to suspend an organisation publicly within the platform.

## 6. Score

**4 / 10** for political and stakeholder readiness of PRD v2.

- Strengths: the honesty rules (formal-network-only labels, public verification, no fake "certificates") and the pilot-before-software approach are strong political insurance. Stage gates give the sponsor an exit that does not look like failure.
