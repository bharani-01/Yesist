# EcoSure — What Changed in v3

**Last updated:** 2026-09-27  
**Inputs:** IEEE YESIST12 problem statement; 36-agent norms and adoption review ([`../research/v2-deep/00-synthesis.md`](../research/v2-deep/00-synthesis.md))

---

## 1. Why v3

v2 scored about 4.6 / 10 against Indian norms and field performance, and matched the problem statement only in part. It tracked sacks from collection onward instead of products from manufacture, kept IoT out of scope, deferred EPR compliance, and had no CPCB view. It also had legal gaps (collectors without standing, batteries, public money flow) found by the 36-agent review.

## 3. Expected score

The 36-agent review estimated that these changes raise the score from 4.6 to about 6.5–7 / 10, and pre-mortem survival from about 30% to about 60%. Verified facts, signed MoUs, and a passed pilot are needed to go higher. These are estimates, not measurements; a fresh review of v3 would confirm them.
